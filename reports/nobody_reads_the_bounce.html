02 — what a player controls

Inside the zone the heading decides. Outside it, nothing.

What a player controls is their heading, and it is worth something only inside a small zone. The lift is the share of rebounds won by attackers arriving rimward — under degrees, measured half a second before the ball is won — minus the share won by everyone else at the same distance. Both halves are drawn on the same axes, because the replication is the argument.

03 — the control

Not proximity relabelled

Players arriving rimward are also nearer the rim, so the lift alone cannot separate heading from proximity. Hold the distance inside a fixed band and vary only the angle: zero degrees is driving at the rim, 180 is running away from it.

04 — the zone a shot earns

Move the shot. The zone moves with it.

Rebounds land close to the rim, and how close depends on where the shot was taken. The zone is , fitted by pinball loss on the calibration half alone and frozen before a held-out game was opened. Click anywhere on the court to shoot from there: the ring is the zone that shot earns, and the dots are the real rebounds off shots within two feet of it.

click the court to move the shot

It does not cover more ground than the flat ft band it replaces: it covers the same share of rebounds evenly across shot distances instead of bunching that coverage at one end. That is the condition it was graded on, and it passed on the held-out games by a wider margin than on the ones it was fitted on.

05 — what the ball says

The ball is barely legible, and what it does say is geometry

The ball's own arrival predicts where it lands, and a pre-registered test of it passed on games never touched. Then this repository refuted what the pass meant. The quantity was the angle the ball arrives at, arctan(drop / run), and only the denominator predicts: how far the ball is still carrying sideways when it reaches rim height, and how far from the rim it gets there. Steepness alone points the other way. Hold those two and the angle keeps nothing.

06 — the contest

Exactly one of ten wins the board

Ten independent probabilities is the wrong structure for a rebound, and ten such numbers do not sum to one. This is the model you played against: a conditional logit over the ten on court, scored half a second before the ball is won, on distance in zone units, heading, speed and whether the player is attacking or defending. The coefficients were fitted on calibration, frozen, committed, and never refitted.

The frozen coefficients

Read this way

A player is better placed the nearer they are in units of the zone their shot earned, the more directly they are heading at the rim, and the faster they are moving. Attackers win about three rebounds in ten, which is what the last coefficient carries. Every feature is a position or a velocity: a definition, never a detector. Nothing here says a player crashed, tagged up or boxed out, and nothing ever will, because tracking cannot see intent. That is SkillCorner's own position, and it closed a whole direction of this project.
